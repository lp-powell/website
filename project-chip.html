CWRUChips
June 2023 to present

DESCRIPTION.... I am the technical lead for CWRUChips as well as the lead engineer for the thin film deposition project. CWRUChips is a student-run design club that is part of the Hackerfab initiative with about 30 members. The goal of the club is to create nanofabrication tools with open-source designs. The club has already constructed a lithostepper and a spincoater. The current club project is thin film deposition with both thermal evaporation and magnetron sputtering.

More information on the team can be found here: cwruhackerfab.netlify.app
